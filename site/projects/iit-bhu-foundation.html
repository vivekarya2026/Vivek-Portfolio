IIT-BHU Foundation

The IIT BHU Foundation is a cornerstone of our alma mater's legacy, connecting generations of graduates and fostering lifelong engagement. This project focused on revitalizing the foundation's online presence and brand identity to better reflect its mission, values, and the dynamic community it serves.

Branding
March 7, 2021
View live project

Introduction

IIT(BHU) foundation is a non-profit body based in New York, USA. It was started by IIT(BHU)'s highly passionate alumni to enable its transformation into a trendsetting pioneer of technical education over the next century. It aims to channelize the benevolence of well established IIT(BHU) alumni towards the development of the institute by capital raising through generours gifts, grants, bequests and the donations of alumni.

LOGO DESIGN PROCESS

‍

Inspiration

The inspiration behind this logo was Mahamana Pandit Madan Mohan Malviya's vision and idea of providing technical education to students that also includes the rich and vast cultural heritage of India. I have tried to potray modern education with BHU’s century old legacy.

‍

‍

Thought Process

The website is mainly targeted at IIT(BHU) alumni. Therefore, I have included the BHU main gate - Lanka gate, that everyone associated with BHU can easily recognize. I have included the sunrise to reflect the dawn of a new beginning and an open book representing the flourishing knowledge from the cultural capital of India.
