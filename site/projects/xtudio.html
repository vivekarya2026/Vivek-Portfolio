Xtudio

Xtudio empowers brands and agencies with pre-built templates and OpenRTB integration, streamlining campaign design and launch for better search ranking.

Product Design
March 1, 2023
View live project
Overview

My journey at Xapads began on the innovation team, working hand-in-hand with a Vp of Innovation to enhance the user experience. We analyzed user behavior and feedback, discovering that the creative process was a major bottleneck for our B2B advertisers. To address this, we initiated a project to streamline creative management and creation, ultimately improving campaign efficiency and reducing costs.

‍

Problem Statement

B2B advertisers struggle with a complex process for creating rich media ad campaigns in the Open RTB market. This project aims to streamline that process by reducing the time and cost involved.

The goal is to make rich media advertising more accessible and efficient for B2B advertisers. Specifically, we aim to:

  1. Decrease rich media creation time by 30%: Make the process faster.
  2. Reduce rich media creative costs by 25%: Save advertisers money.
  3. Improve user experience score by 20%: Make the platform easier to use.
  4. Enhance platform intuitiveness and efficiency: Streamline the workflow.

By achieving these goals, we'll help B2B advertisers launch effective rich media campaigns more quickly and affordably.

‍

Users and Audience

Our product is designed for digital advertising professionals who want to streamline and optimize the creation, delivery, and analysis of rich media ad campaigns.

This includes:

  • B2B Advertisers
  • Rich Media Ad Designers
  • Ad Campaign Managers

Key Research findings, categorized into key pain points:

Campaign Creation & Management:

  • Targeting: Difficulty creating precise B2B audience segments within Open RTB.
  • Budgeting: Lack of fine-grained control over budget allocation across exchanges.
  • Data: Challenges integrating client data with Open RTB platforms for targeting.
  • Reporting: Missing B2B-specific insights (lead gen, brand awareness).
  • Attribution: Hard to attribute conversions to specific rich media ad placements.

‍

Rich Media Creative Development:

  • Technical Specs: Complexities navigating specs across ad exchanges and devices.
  • Design Constraints: Limitations from ad exchange sizes and file formats.
  • Interactivity: Difficulty implementing desired interactive features within constraints.
  • Cross-Device: Ensuring consistent user experience on all devices.
  • Approval: Lengthy internal and ad exchange approval processes.

These insights directly informed our product goals and helped us prioritize features that would best address user pain points and enhance their overall experience. This data validated the demand for Xtudio and highlighted specific features that would give us a competitive advantage.

Step 2- Define

By integrating insights from both qualitative and quantitative research, we were able to define clear, data-driven goals for Xtudio's development.  

These goals will guide our decision-making throughout the design process, ensuring that we create a product that not only solves real user problems but also thrives in the market.

Our Key Challenges Addressed:
  • Complexity:  B2B advertisers face a fragmented and time-consuming process for rich media ad creation in the Open RTB market.
  • Cost:  High production costs can limit the use of engaging rich media formats.
  • Ease of Use:  Existing platforms often lack intuitive interfaces and efficient workflows.

Step -3 Design Solution

Our platform was designed to solve these specific pain points B2B advertisers face in the Open RTB market. Here's how we tackled each stage to achieve our goals:

‍

1. Simplified Account & Brand Onboarding:

  • Streamlined Registration:  Simplified account creation to get advertisers started quickly.
  • Brand Asset Library:  Centralized storage for logos, images, and videos to eliminate the need for constant re-uploads and reduce creative asset search time.
  • Why?  Faster onboarding means quicker time to campaign launch, addressing the goal of decreasing creation time.

‍

2. Intuitive Campaign Creation:

  • Guided Campaign Setup: Step-by-step process to define target audiences, goals, and budgets clearly.
  • Why?  A structured approach reduces errors and makes campaign planning more efficient, contributing to time savings.

‍

3. Rich Media Creation Made Easy:

  • Customizable Templates: A library of professionally designed templates that adhere to brand guidelines.
  • Drag-and-Drop Interface:  A user-friendly way to add interactive elements (videos, forms, etc.) without coding knowledge.
  • Why?  Templates cut down design time significantly, while the drag-and-drop interface lowers the barrier to entry for creating rich media, directly impacting both time and cost goals.

‍

4. Real-Time Preview & Collaboration:

  • Multi-Device Previews: See how ads look on various devices to catch display issues early.
  • Feedback & Revision Tools:  Built-in commenting to streamline the approval process.
  • Why? These features minimize back-and-forth communication, reducing the need for revisions and speeding up campaign readiness, thus improving the user experience score.

‍

5. Data-Driven Optimization:

  • Smart Dashboard: At-a-glance view of key campaign metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions).
  • Automated Reporting: Customizable reports for easy performance tracking.
  • Why?  Data-driven insights empower advertisers to make quick adjustments, maximizing campaign ROI and further addressing the goal of cost reduction.

Outcomes and Takeaways

The project delivered exceptional results, exceeding many of our initial expectations:

  • Streamlined Rich Media Creation:  We significantly reduced the time it takes to produce high-quality rich media content, leading to faster campaign launches and increased agility.
  • Substantial Cost Savings: Our investment in this project paid off handsomely, with rich media creative costs dropping significantly more than anticipated. This allows us to allocate budget to other critical initiatives.
  • Enhanced User Experience: The project resulted in a substantial improvement in user experience metrics, confirming that our customers are more engaged and satisfied with our content than ever before.

‍

Key Learnings
  • User-Centric Design:  Recognized the need for personalized approaches due to diverse user personas
  • Importance of Research:  Emphasized the value of extensive user research and testing to guide design decisions
  • Collaboration:  While I was the only dedicated designer, our cross-functional team fostered a collaborative environment. This allowed us to leverage each other's expertise and deliver a successful pump monitoring and control system that met user needs and business objectives.

‍

Challenges & Areas for Improvement
  • Complexity Underestimation: Underestimated the technical knowledge required for rich media ad creation
  • User Education: Identified the need for more simplified platform features and additional educational resources

‍

Next Steps & Future Focus
  • Enhanced User Education:  Invest in creating comprehensive resources to guide users through rich media ad creation
  • Continuous Refinement: Further improve platform intuitiveness and user-centric features based on ongoing feedback
