Schoolpal

The Microsoft Design Challenge 2022 tasked participants with addressing real-world problems through innovative design solutions. I chose to tackle the issue of disengagement in online classes and the growing disparity between online and offline learning experiences.

UX Design
April 24, 2022
View live project

Why this Problem

After the pandemic, the education sector has grown exponentially with the help of technology. But primary teachers in India (expected to grow up to 30 lac in 2030) face many issues due to this digitalisation.

The majority of the primary teachers do not have access to laptops and are struggling with digital learning platforms. As a result, they are unable to teach the students effectively.

‍

Due to this paradigm shift in the teaching mode, primary teachers find it challenging to engage the students

‍

Phase 2

User Persona

I created the following user personas based on user interviews and surveys to make design decisions.

Identified Problem

‍

Problem Statement

How to make online classes more engaging and bridge the gap between online and offline modes of teaching?

‍

‍

The reason why I chose this problem?

I have been a passionate student who still cherishes my school days and the stories my English and Hindi teachers taught me. I do not want future students to miss this experience. Therefore, I want to solve this problem.

‍

‍

Problem break down

‍

1. Complex user flow in existing apps

Teachers find it difficult to track the students, their performance and all the respective classes they teach. There is steep learning curve beacuse of complex UI.

‍

2. Poor engagement with student

Not all the students attend class and some are not responsive. Although few tools are already present on these apps but they are not designed for educational classroom purposes. Thus teachers find it hard to engage everyone in the class as they already have a limited time dedicated to their subject.

‍

3. Weak emapthy link with student

Teachers are not able to connect on a personal level with the student as they are not able to interact equally with them.

‍

Phase 3: Ideation

‍

Brainstroming

‍

From the problem identified. I diversified my thinking to brainstorm different directions of ideas that can be implemented

‍

‍

Let's Fix The Mess by

‍

1. Subject-wise analysis

Data-driven reports will give teachers a view of students' strong and weak topics based on assignment and quiz results.

‍

2. Attendance report

Comprehensive attendance data for the teacher to know if the student frequently misses classes or takes long leaves.

‍

3. Connect with parent

Teachers can analyze reports and discuss with parents what problems the child is facing and how they can together work that out.

‍

4. Building empathy

With emotional connections and personalized insights, an empathetic connection would develop among the teachers and their students, helping for a smooth shift from online to the hybrid classroom and vice versa.

‍

5. Uncomplicated UI

The application is kept simple and intuitive for a shallow learning curve. Even the teacher new to online teaching practices can easily get used to the application.

‍

6. Gamifying Interactions

Introducing the points for online polls, quizzes, and class engagement will help the teacher to get the students engage with the course and be more attentive in class

Conclusion

‍

SchoolPal comes with a vision to fill existing solutions' gaps.The comprehensive reports help the teacher understand more about their students. A personal connection will build over connect and will help to strengthen empathy based on the insights and data.The simplistic UI approach will help the teacher to get quickly onboarded and get the most out of the resources. Gamified rewards for engagement with class content, quizzes, polls will get the student to interact and be more attentive than ever. We aim to make online and hybrid teaching more fulfilling.
