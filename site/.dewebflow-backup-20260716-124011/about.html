My Story
About me

Product Designer based in New Delhi

Hey Visitor, I'm Vivek Arya, born and raised in Roorkee, my journey from ABN School inside IIT Roorkee to IIT Varanasi in 2018 was fueled by curiosity, self-learning, and a dash of generative AI. The college opened doors to diverse experiences, leading to the co-founding of Happy Minds, a mental wellness startup. My role as product designer and manager honed my skills in UX, UI, and product development, culminating in a successful acquisition.

Navigating COVID challenges, I gained valuable insights through internships, which proved instrumental in Happy Minds' success. My passion for healthcare and education led me to Xapads Media, where I tackled innovative products like Xtudio and Chatie. With 4 years of experience and a knack for creative problem-solving, I'm eager to contribute my skills to projects that make a real difference in people's lives.
