Acquisition & Evolution: From Happy Minds to WellM

In my final year of college, Happy Minds, the mental wellness app I co-founded, was acquired by WellM, a leading digital health platform. This marked a pivotal moment in the app's journey. While the core features and mission of promoting mental well-being remained intact, the app underwent a transformation. It was rebranded as WellM, aligning with the parent company's identity, and received a visual overhaul to create a more cohesive user experience within the broader WellM ecosystem. This acquisition not only validated the impact of Happy Minds but also expanded its reach and potential to positively influence a wider audience.

Challenges & Conclusion

‍

Yes, not everything went as planned

‍

‍

1. Daily journal failed!

‍

Users wanted voice chat along with text messaging.Users didn’t use the daily journal as often as they should. They generally do not know what to write in the bulletin and how to express their emotions. They felt alone in the struggle, and updating the journal wasn’t helping.

We started researching user behavior and psychology to improve the activity to make the journaling process effortless and interactive. So our team decided to introduce a community feature for the user to express their feeling anonymously, which will help them get out of the prevalent social stigma.

‍

‍

2. Users wanted voice chat alongwith text messaging.

‍

During the pandemic, everyone was locked up in their homes. Due to small houses in India, some users weren’t able to talk to therapists. And with text messaging, it became cumbersome to type such long messages. To tackle this, I proposed to launch a voice-enabled messaging feature. So that users can create and store a voice message whenever they are comfortable and can share it with the therapists during the session.
